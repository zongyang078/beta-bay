About the Meetup

Beta Bay is a weekly climbing meetup for anyone who loves getting vertical in the Bay Area. Whether you're flashing V6s or just learning to trust your feet, you're welcome here. We rotate between local gyms and outdoor spots every week, bringing together climbers of all levels to share beta, build community, and have a good time on the wall. No membership required — just show up and climb.

What to Expect

Climb Together, Get Stronger

  • All Levels Welcome: From first-timers to seasoned crushers, everyone has a spot on the wall.
  • Rotating Locations: We switch between gyms and outdoor crags so you never get bored.
  • Free Beta: Experienced climbers are always around to share tips and route advice.

Locations

We climb at these spots around the Bay Area:

  • Movement Climbing Gym — Sunnyvale. Great for bouldering and top rope.
  • Planet Granite — Belmont. Old-school gym with a strong community vibe.
  • Castle Rock State Park — Classic Bay Area outdoor bouldering on real rock.

Safety & Requirements

  • Climbing shoes required (rentals available at gyms)
  • Harness and belay certification needed for top rope sessions
  • Outdoor sessions require a signed waiver and a helmet
  • Beginners welcome — experienced climbers will be on-site to help
  • Bring water, chalk, and a good attitude
